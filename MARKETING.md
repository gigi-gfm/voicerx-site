# GFM — Path to $10k/mo MRR

Updated 2026-05-16.

## The number

$10k/mo from GFM products & services. Pick one path and run it:

| Path | Price | Customers needed |
|---|---|---|
| A. Premium VoiceRx | $200/mo | 50 clinicians |
| B. Standard VoiceRx | $99/mo | 101 clinicians |
| C. Mixed: 30 paid + practice services | $200 + $4k services | 30 clinicians + services |

**Default: Path A.** Higher price = fewer logos to close = less support load = faster signal. Solo & small-group primary care physicians who pay themselves and feel charting pain personally.

## Differentiator (use this everywhere)

Built by a practicing family-medicine MD (Dr. Garcia), for primary care. Not a Silicon Valley scribe retrofitted. HIPAA BAA on day one.

## What to work on, in order

### Phase 1 — Make it buyable (weeks 1–2)

- [ ] Landing page at a clean domain (voicerx.md or similar), not garciafx-dictation.html
- [ ] One headline, one demo video (60s, Dr. Garcia screen + voice), one pricing tile, one CTA: "Start 30-day free trial"
- [ ] Live signup → trial provisioning (even if onboarding is manual behind the scenes)
- [ ] BAA download link in footer (already have the PDF)
- [ ] Stripe or similar — $200/mo, annual discount, cancel anytime
- [ ] GA4 + a single conversion event: `trial_started`

**Done = a stranger can find the page, sign up, and start dictating without you touching anything.**

### Phase 2 — First 10 paying customers (weeks 3–8)

These come from **founder-led outreach, not ads.** Ads are wasted until you've talked to 50 prospects.

- [ ] Build a list of 200 target physicians: solo/2-provider primary care in TX/your state. Sources: state medical board roster, AAFP directory, LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- [ ] LinkedIn DM template: physician-to-physician, 3 sentences, ask for 15 min not a sale
- [ ] Goal: 10 demos/week → 2 trials/week → 1 paid conversion/week
- [ ] Concierge onboarding: you personally set up each trial in a 20-min call
- [ ] After 14 days, ask each trial user: "Would you be sad if this went away?" — record the verbatim

**Done = 10 paying customers, 5 of whom will give you a testimonial on video.**

### Phase 3 — Repeatable acquisition (weeks 9–16)

- [ ] Comparison pages: "VoiceRx vs Freed", "VoiceRx vs Suki", "VoiceRx vs Heidi". Each ranks for the competitor's brand + "alternative"
- [ ] 2 blog posts/week on real physician questions: "How much time does an AI scribe save?", "Is ambient AI HIPAA compliant?", "Best AI scribe for solo practice"
- [ ] Dr. Garcia on LinkedIn: 3 posts/week. Charting pain, AI in primary care, before/after notes (de-identified). Founder-MD voice is the moat
- [ ] Referral: existing user gets 1 free month per referral that converts to paid
- [ ] Google Ads pilot, $1.5k budget, only on high-intent terms: "ai medical scribe", "ambient ai scribe", "[competitor] alternative". Kill any keyword over $40 CPA after 30 conversions

**Done = at least 30% of new trials come from non-outreach channels.**

### Phase 4 — Get to $10k (weeks 17–26)

- [ ] One channel partnership: state AFP chapter, a billing company, or an EHR add-on marketplace
- [ ] Annual plan push: 2 months free if paid upfront (improves cash + locks in)
- [ ] Case study with quantified savings (hours/week, $/month) for 3 customers
- [ ] Hire one part-time SDR or VA to run the outreach list you built in Phase 2

## What NOT to do

- Don't rebrand. Don't redesign the app. Don't add features no customer asked for.
- Don't run ads before you have 10 paying customers — you'll learn nothing.
- Don't chase enterprise/health-system deals yet — 9-month sales cycles will kill you.
- Don't post on 5 social platforms. LinkedIn only until it's working.
- Don't write a white paper, a podcast, or a Substack. None of those move MRR at this stage.

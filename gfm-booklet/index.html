A former music teacher turned physician, Dr. Theresa “Tess” Garcia brings rare warmth, patience, and cultural understanding to medicine. As a Mexican-American and Native-American doctor — and the first woman physician in Grain Valley — she has spent her career caring for families, veterans, and the people too often overlooked by rushed, traditional healthcare.

She built Garcia Family Medicine on a simple idea: healthcare should feel personal again. No phone trees. No gatekeepers. No ten-minute appointments. Just a doctor who knows your name, answers your text, and has the time to truly listen.

Her commitment isn’t only professional — it’s personal. Dr. Tess lost 80 pounds herself using the very same evidence-based approach she now offers her weight-management patients. She doesn’t just prescribe the journey; she’s walked it.

Service 03 · Pelvic Health

CoreLift™ Pelvic Floor Therapy

CoreLift / EMSELLA treatment room & device
images/corelift.jpg

Bladder leaks and pelvic-floor weakness are common — and quietly life-limiting. CoreLift offers a comfortable, non-invasive way to rebuild that core strength, no surgery and no downtime.

CoreLift uses FDA-cleared EMSELLA technology to deliver thousands of pelvic-floor contractions in a single session — the equivalent of thousands of Kegels — while you sit comfortably and fully clothed.

Each session is just 28 minutes. The typical protocol is six sessions over three weeks, and 95% of patients report meaningful improvement in bladder control and quality of life.

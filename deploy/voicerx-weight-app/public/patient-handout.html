Your Weight-Management Diary
A simple app to track your meals, sleep, and movement — right on your phone.
Scan with your camera

Get started in seconds

Point your phone's camera at the code, or type the address below into your browser. Then add it to your home screen so it opens like an app — and works even without signal.

voicerxweightapp1.pages.dev

📲 Install it

1
Scan the code (or open the link) in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.
2
Add to Home Screen:
iPhone — tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android — tap the Add to home screen prompt (or menu ⋮).
3
Open it from the new icon. Enter your name and the goals you set with Dr. Garcia on the Me tab.

📖 Use it every day

  • 🍽️
    Meals — jot down breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks & water.
  • 😴
    Sleep — hours and how rested you felt.
  • 🏃
    Movement — what you did and for how long.
  • 📊
    Check your week on the Summary tab for your tally & progress.
  • 📤
    Send it to us — tap Send my diary to the clinic before your visit.

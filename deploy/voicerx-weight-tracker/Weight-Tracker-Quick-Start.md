# Weight Tracker — Quick Start

A simple way to track your weight-management patients' **meals, sleep, and
movement** — and let patients send their own logs in. There are two apps:

| App | Who | What |
|-----|-----|------|
| **Weight Tracker** | You (clinician) | Your roster of patients + their diaries, on your device |
| **Weight Diary** | Patients | Their own daily diary, with a "Send to the clinic" button |

> **Everything stays on the device it's used on.** Nothing is uploaded; there's
> no login. Keep your device passcode-locked and **back up regularly**
> (see below). These apps support care — they are **not** the medical record.

---

## 1) Put the Tracker on your phone (once)

1. Open the Tracker link in **Chrome (Android)** or **Safari (iPhone)**.
2. Add it to your home screen:
   - **Android:** tap the **Add to home screen** prompt, or menu ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*.
   - **iPhone:** **Share** → *Add to Home Screen*.
3. Open it from the home-screen icon — it now works full-screen and **offline**.

---

## 2) Add a patient

1. Tap **＋** (top right).
2. Enter the **name** (required). Optionally add DOB, MRN, starting weight, height.
3. Set the **weekly goals** with the patient — calorie/portion plan, sleep
   hours, movement minutes, water. (Defaults: 7.5 h sleep, 30 min, 8 glasses.)
4. **Save** — they appear on your roster.

---

## 3) Log and review a week

- Tap a patient → **Diary** tab. Fill in each day: breakfast / lunch / dinner /
  snacks, water, mood, weigh-in, **sleep** (hours, quality, times), and
  **movement** (activity, minutes, steps, intensity).
- Use **‹ / ›** to move between weeks — each week is saved separately.
- **Summary** tab shows the **weekly tally** (total movement, average sleep /
  water / steps, "X / 7 days hit goal"), plus **weight** and **movement**
  trends over time.
- **🖨** prints/saves the current week as a PDF for the chart.

---

## 4) Let patients log on their own phones

Give patients the **Weight Diary** app link (it can be personalized, e.g.
`…/?patient=David%20Martin`). They install it the same way and log daily.

When they're ready to share with you:

1. Patient taps **📤 Send my diary to the clinic** (on the Diary app's **Me** tab).
2. They pick **email / message / AirDrop** (or it saves a file to attach).
3. You receive the small `.json` file and save it to the device running the Tracker.
4. In the Tracker, tap **⬇ Import a patient's shared week** → choose the file.
   - It matches the patient by name (creating them if new), merges their weeks,
     and updates goals/starting weight. Re-importing **updates**, never duplicates.

---

## 5) Back up (do this often)

Because the data lives only on your device:

- **⬆ Back up all data** → saves one file with every patient and week. Keep it
  somewhere safe (email it to yourself, save to Drive, etc.).
- **↺ Restore from a backup file** → brings everything back on a new/reset device.
- **🗑 Erase everything** wipes the device clean (back up first!).

---

## ✅ Tips
- Open from the **home-screen icon**, not from an in-app browser (Messages/Gmail), so install + offline work.
- iPhone: data persists best when opened from the installed icon and the app is used regularly.
- Print a week to PDF to drop into the chart at AtlasMD.
- Back up after each clinic day — there is no cloud copy.

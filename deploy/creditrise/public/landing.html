⚖️ An honest word about credit repair

CreditRise is an educational self-help tool — not a credit-repair organization, law firm, or financial advisor. It helps you exercise rights you already have under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, for free.

It cannot and does not guarantee any score increase or the removal of accurate information. Accurate, verifiable debts generally stay on your report. Anyone promising to erase legitimate debt is not being straight with you.

If you plan to charge for this tool, keep it on the education/software side of the law and have an attorney review CROA and your state's rules before you launch.

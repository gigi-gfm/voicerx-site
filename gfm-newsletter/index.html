Care that knows your name

At Garcia Family Medicine, you’re a person, not a chart number. Led by Dr. Theresa “Tess” Garcia, MD, FAAFP, our practice is built around unhurried visits, real access to your physician, and care for the whole family — from childhood checkups to managing the conditions that come with every stage of life.

Spotlight · Healthy Weight

Weight is health — and it’s treatable.

Dr. Garcia is board-certified in Obesity Medicine (DABOM). Medical weight management goes far beyond willpower: it looks at metabolism, sleep, stress, medications, and nutrition together, with a plan made for you. If your weight has felt stuck, that’s a medical conversation worth having.

By the numbers

5 health facts worth knowing

1

150 minutes of moderate activity a week — about 20–30 min a day — is the standard adult target, plus muscle-strengthening twice weekly.

2

High blood pressure has no symptoms. Normal is generally under 120/80 mmHg. The only way to know yours is to check it.

3

7+ hours of sleep a night is what most adults need. Short sleep is linked to heart disease, weight gain, and low mood.

4

Half your plate fruits and vegetables is a simple rule that quietly improves fiber, blood sugar, and blood pressure.

5

Most colon cancer is preventable. Routine screening for average-risk adults now starts at age 45. Ask if you’re due.

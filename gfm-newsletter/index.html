Summer Health

Sunshine, cookouts, and long days outside — summer is the best. A few smart habits keep it that way. Here’s your quick guide to beating the heat, dodging allergies, and handling stings & rashes — brought to you by Dr. Tess and the GFM team.

Beat the Heat

Heat Illness

When the body can’t cool itself, trouble builds fast. Knowing the two stages can save a life.

Heat Exhaustion
  • Heavy sweating, cold/clammy skin
  • Dizziness, headache, nausea
  • Weak, fast pulse · muscle cramps
Do this

Move to cool/shade, sip water, loosen clothing, apply cool wet cloths. Rest until it passes.

Heat Stroke
  • Temp 103°F+ · hot, red skin
  • Confusion or fainting
  • Strong, racing pulse
⚠ Call 911 now

A true emergency. Cool aggressively; don’t give fluids if they’re not fully alert.

Summer Allergies

Grass pollen, mold, and smog peak in the warm months — sneezing, itchy eyes, and congestion follow.

Calm the symptoms
  • Check pollen counts; stay in on high days
  • Shower & change after time outdoors
  • Windows closed, A/C on at peak
  • Antihistamines — ask us what fits you

Bee & Wasp Stings

Scrape the stinger out sideways (don’t pinch — that squeezes in more venom). Wash, then cold compress.

Ease it
  • Ice 10 min on/off for swelling
  • Antihistamine for itch, ibuprofen for pain
⚠ Call 911 if

Lips/tongue/throat swell, hives spread, or breathing gets hard — use an EpiPen if prescribed.

Heat Rash & Prickly Skin

Skin

Trapped sweat plugs the skin’s ducts, leaving tiny red, prickly bumps — usually in folds, on the neck, chest, or where clothing rubs. Common in babies and on muggy days.

Soothe & prevent

Cool down and keep skin dry. Choose loose, breathable cotton, skip heavy creams/ointments, and use a fan or A/C. It usually clears on its own in a few days — see us if it blisters, weeps, or won’t fade.

☀ Summer Factoids
#1

Heat is the deadliest weather hazard in the U.S. — more deaths most years than floods, tornadoes & hurricanes.

10×

A car can heat up ~20°F in 10 minutes. Never leave kids or pets — not even “just a sec.”

1.5L

You can sweat out over a liter an hour in heat. Thirst lags behind — drink before you feel it.

15 min

Unprotected skin can burn in 15 minutes. UV peaks 10am–4pm — reapply SPF every 2 hrs.

💧

Pee check: pale-yellow = hydrated. Dark = drink up. Simplest hydration gauge there is.

Smart & Safe Summer Activities

Make the most of the season — a few easy habits keep the fun going.

Hydrate early & oftenCarry water; add fruit or a pinch of electrolytes on sweaty days.
Time it rightSave hard exercise & yard work for early morning or evening, out of peak sun.
SPF 30+, reapplyEvery 2 hours and after swimming or sweating. Hat & shades help too.
Water safetyNever swim alone; watch kids closely; learn the signs of quiet drowning.
Bug-smartRepellent for mosquitoes & ticks; check for ticks after trails & tall grass.
Cookout careKeep cold food cold; don’t let plates sit out over 2 hrs (1 hr if 90°F+).
Know your medsSome prescriptions raise sun & heat sensitivity — ask Dr. Tess if yours do.
Hot pavement checkIf it’s too hot for your hand for 5 sec, it’s too hot for bare feet & paws.
Did You Know?

🐝 Bees die after one sting; wasps don’t. Wasps & hornets can sting again and again — back away calmly, don’t swat.

🌡️ Humidity makes heat worse. Sweat can’t evaporate in muggy air, so your body’s main cooling system stalls.

🐾 Hooch’s tip: dogs cool by panting, not sweating. Shade, water, and cool ground keep our Comfort Specialist happy.

🍉 Eat your water. Watermelon, cucumber & berries are ~90% water — tasty hydration that beats the heat.
